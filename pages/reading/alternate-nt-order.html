Alternate New Testament Book Order

This page offers an alternative book ordering for the New Teatament. It mostly follows the book order of the Westcott and Hort Greek New Testament, with the exception of the Gospels. For the order of the Gospels, they follow apostolic ordering, which places the two apostles before the two evangelists. Notice also that the Letter to the Hebrews is placed after Paul's letters to the curches and before his pastoral letters.
